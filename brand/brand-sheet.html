One mark for the whole Rhema family.

The Block R is built from three solid pieces: a stem, a bowl and a leg that leans forward. It reads as a letter first, looks like a software company, and holds up at 16 pixels.

Recommended: the Block R

The three blocks stand for the building blocks of software. The leg leans forward to match the line "Technology that moves you forward".

Gradient tile: the AI product

Rhema Intelligence uses the blue to purple tile with a white R. The gradient marks it as the AI product.

Navy tile: the company

Rhema Solutions uses a flat navy tile with a light-blue leg. It is calm enough to sit beside the other products.

No tile: for text and documents

On plain light or dark backgrounds, use the bare R with an accent leg. Files for both are included.

Holds up small

The stem and bowl are heavy enough that the R still reads at the 16 pixel size of a browser tab.

Concepts we passed on

Arrow R

  • An R whose leg is an arrow, tied to the "forward" idea.
  • The arrow points down and to the right, so it reads as "download" more than "progress".
  • The thin bowl also gets weak below 32 px.

Hexagon R

  • A rounded R inside a hexagon, a familiar shape for tech brands.
  • Clear and friendly, but it looks like many other platforms.
  • The hexagon does not say anything about Rhema.

  • Set the wordmark in Inter: "Rhema" in ExtraBold with tight spacing, and "Solutions" or "Intelligence" in Medium.
  • Keep clear space around the mark equal to one quarter of its width.
  • Do not use the mark below 16 px, or the full lockup below 120 px wide.
  • Do not stretch the mark, change the leg to another colour, or put the gradient tile on a blue or purple background.
