Rhema Solutions builds practical software products and digital solutions for businesses and organisations around the world.

Who we are

A software company that ships its own products

We are based in India and build for clients and customers worldwide. Alongside client projects, we design and run our own products for churches, gyms, Vacation Bible School teams and growing businesses.

Building and supporting our own software keeps us honest. We know what it takes to launch, to keep a system running and to answer a customer who is stuck.

Principles

How we approach the work

Practical first
We start with the task people do every day and make that task faster, not with a feature list.
Built to be used
Clear screens, sensible defaults and software that works on a phone as well as a desktop.
Careful with data
Roles, audit trails and protected sign-in come as standard, because the data is your customers' and members' data.
Made to last
Documented APIs, automated tests and plain, maintainable code, so you can keep building after we hand over.
